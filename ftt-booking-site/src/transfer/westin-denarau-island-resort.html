The route

Nadi Airport to Westin Denarau

From Nadi International Airport, the drive is about 12 km via Queens Road and Denarau Road. Allow 18-22 minutes in normal traffic.

Practical notes for arrival

  • Drop-off is at the dedicated Westin lobby (separate from the Sheraton lobby, despite the shared driveway).
  • The Westin has its own private beach section and pool — your driver can advise the closest entrance based on which villa you're booked into.
  • Guests with children should book the Sheraton instead — the Westin's adults-pool policy means kids can't use the main pool.
