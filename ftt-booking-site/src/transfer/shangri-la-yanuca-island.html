The route

Nadi Airport to Shangri-La Yanuca

From Nadi International Airport, the drive is approximately 72 km — about 1 hour 16 minutes via Queens Road south. The route passes Sigatoka Town and the Sand Dunes National Park.

Practical notes for arrival

  • Drop-off is at the resort lobby. The peninsula layout means buggy transport between lobby and beach blocks is normal.
  • Three distinct beaches: family beach (north), main beach (centre), adults-only beach (south) — your driver can advise on the closest entry point if you know your room block.
  • Coral Coast traffic is generally light, but the Sigatoka River bridge can bottleneck during morning sugar truck convoys (June-November harvest season).
