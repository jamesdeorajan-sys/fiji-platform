The route

Nadi Airport to Marriott Momi Bay

From Nadi International Airport, the drive is approximately 42 km — about 52 minutes via Queens Road south through Sigatoka district. The route passes coastal villages and sugarcane country.

Practical notes for arrival

  • Drop-off is at the resort's grand lobby with floor-to-ceiling ocean views.
  • Overwater bure guests are escorted via golf cart from the lobby — confirm with reception which jetty your bure is on (north or south).
  • The Momi Bay road is sealed but has some narrow sections — your driver routes around the slowest stretches.
  • Honeymooners often request a stop at the Sleeping Giant gardens en route — add this to special requests if interested (small extra time).
