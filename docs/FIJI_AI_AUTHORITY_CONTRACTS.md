# Fiji AI Authority Contracts

**Status:** Normative design. No implementation or production authority is granted.

## Lagi boundary

Lagi may understand traveler intent; ask clarifying questions; retrieve approved Fiji knowledge; compare suitable options; request authoritative fare quotes and booking creation; retrieve permitted booking status; generate itineraries; route leads; draft customer communications; and use consented traveler preferences.

Lagi may not invent prices; maintain competing fare tables in prompts; claim availability without an authoritative result; claim a booking exists before Booking API persistence; change booking status or assign drivers directly; claim payment success; expose private traveler or partner data; or treat generated prose as operational truth. It must fail closed when authority is unavailable.

## Contract registry

## Cross-service rules

Service outputs include schema and record versions, authoritative timestamps, stable status/reason codes, and trace identifiers. Consumer-friendly prose is never the authoritative payload. Least privilege and field-level minimization apply. Prompts contain instructions and identifiers, not operational secrets or executable pricing tables.

Booking API publication remains a blocking dependency. WhatsApp remains only a communication channel. The first commercial release remains very small, and Fare Service Phase 2 is not yet authorized.

## Promotion ownership split

The Promotion Service is the system of record for campaign identity, terms, audience, effective window, funding owner, budgets, limits, approval status, lifecycle, eligibility, and redemption state. Only the commercial promotions owner may propose lifecycle changes, and James approves any activation gate affecting a release. The Promotion Service never calculates a final fare. It returns an idempotent, versioned eligibility/redemption decision; the Fare Service never invents campaign eligibility and exclusively applies the approved monetary effect to a price and quote. Neither service may silently reproduce the other's rules. Redemption and quote evidence records the campaign/version, eligibility and redemption IDs, fare calculation ID, quote ID, actor, time, and idempotency key. A failed or indeterminate promotion check never produces a guessed discount.

## Identity and consent models

A traveler has one stable opaque subject ID with separately access-controlled contact methods, verified attributes, and optional profile links; channel identifiers such as a WhatsApp number are aliases, not identity truth. Partner and operator identities consist of an organization ID, a separately identifiable verified human or workload subject, role, delegated scopes, assurance level, and effective membership. The Identity Service owns authentication and session identity, cross-service actor identifiers, identity linking and deliberate separation; linking, unlinking, recovery, and role changes require its authoritative workflows and must never merge distinct travelers merely because they share contact details.

The Consent Ledger, not a prompt, session, or profile field, owns grants, purpose, scope, source, effective time, expiry, and revocation. Revocation creates an immutable event, takes effect for new processing immediately, invalidates or narrows cached authorization and downstream optional use, and emits a versioned invalidation notice to affected services. Required legal retention may preserve restricted evidence but does not authorize continued optional processing. Identity and consent audit evidence records actor, subject, purpose, scope, decision, assurance, notice/version, source, effective/expiry/revocation time, correlation ID, and access outcome without placing credentials or secrets in logs. The Traveler Profile Service may store consented preferences but cannot replace Identity Service records or Consent Ledger evidence.

## Booking lifecycle authority

The Booking Service owns booking state; Payment, Dispatch, and Communication Services own their separate state machines. A consumer must never collapse these states into a single “confirmed” flag.

| Authority | States | Allowed transitions and responsible actors |
|---|---|---|
| Booking Service | `BOOKING_CREATED`, `PENDING_CONFIRMATION`, `CONFIRMED`, `ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED` | After explicit traveler or authorized human confirmation, Booking may persist `BOOKING_CREATED→PENDING_CONFIRMATION`; Booking orchestration may apply `→CONFIRMED` only with valid quote and required availability; an authoritative Dispatch event permits `CONFIRMED→ASSIGNED`; verified service events permit `ASSIGNED→IN_PROGRESS→COMPLETED`. Traveler or authorized booking agent may request amendment or cancellation; authorized booking operations may transition any eligible nonterminal state to `CANCELLED`. Material amendments require a replacement quote before confirmation. |
| Payment Service | `NOT_REQUIRED`, `PENDING`, `AUTHORIZED`, `PAID`, `FAILED`, `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`, `REFUNDED` | With explicit traveler or authorized human confirmation, Payment may record `NOT_REQUIRED` or initiate `PENDING`; only provider-backed results permit `PENDING→AUTHORIZED`, `AUTHORIZED→PAID`, or `PENDING/AUTHORIZED→FAILED`; authorized finance operations may apply `PAID→PARTIALLY_REFUNDED→REFUNDED` or `PAID→REFUNDED`. |
| Dispatch Service | `UNASSIGNED`, `PROPOSED`, `ASSIGNED`, `ACCEPTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED` | Booking confirmation permits `UNASSIGNED→PROPOSED`; an authorized dispatcher or approved deterministic dispatcher applies `PROPOSED→ASSIGNED`; the assigned operator/driver applies `ASSIGNED→ACCEPTED→IN_PROGRESS→COMPLETED`; an eligible booking cancellation or authorized dispatcher permits a nonterminal `→CANCELLED`. |
| Communication Service | `QUEUED`, `SENT`, `DELIVERED`, `READ`, `FAILED`, `RECEIVED` | An authorized service or agent may create `QUEUED`; provider evidence permits `QUEUED→SENT→DELIVERED→READ` or a nonterminal outbound `→FAILED`; authenticated inbound-provider evidence creates `RECEIVED`. Communication records never transition other authority states. |

Every transition validates current state, actor role, authority result, version, and idempotency key. Reuse of a key with the same normalized request returns the original result; reuse with different content returns an auditable conflict and performs no mutation. An expired quote blocks confirmation and any material amendment until Fare and Quote authorities issue a replacement accepted by the traveler. Quotes, leads, payment attempts, WhatsApp messages, and dispatch records cannot independently constitute a booking; only Booking API persistence creates one.

Compensating rules preserve evidence: if booking persistence fails, no booking is claimed and any payment authorization is reversed through an authorized payment operation or escalated; if required payment fails, booking cannot advance to the payment-dependent confirmed path and dispatch is not released; if dispatch work fails after confirmation, the booking remains persisted and enters authorized human exception handling without inventing a state; if communication fails, authoritative states remain unchanged and retry/escalation is recorded; if amendment or cancellation partially fails, the prior state and immutable request remain visible until an authorized reconciler completes or reverses it. Automated compensation is idempotent, attributable, and never fabricates success.
