## 1. What Vakaviti is

Vakaviti is a directory and enquiry channel for Fiji tourism operators — tours, activities, and
ground transport. We help travellers discover real operators and connect with them directly. We are
not a travel agency, and (as of this draft) we do not process payments or confirm bookings on
anyone's behalf.

## 2. Listings and verification status

Every operator/product listing shows one of these statuses, and the meaning of each is fixed by how
the system actually works — not by marketing language:

- **"Publicly listed"** — Vakaviti found public evidence the business exists. This does **not** mean
  Vakaviti has verified the business's identity, licences, prices, or availability.
- **"Vakaviti Verified"** — a human at Vakaviti has completed an evidence-based review confirming
  identity and key facts. This is never granted automatically or by AI alone.
- **"Vakaviti Pilot Partner" / "Vakaviti-discovered"** — intermediate states reflecting how a listing
  was created and whether the operator has confirmed involvement.

Vakaviti makes **no guarantee** that any listed price, availability, or detail is current or accurate
unless the listing is marked Vakaviti Verified, and even then, always confirm directly with the
operator before relying on any detail for travel planning.

## 3. How enquiries work

Tapping "Ask Vakaviti on WhatsApp" opens a WhatsApp conversation. During this preview, every enquiry
is routed to a single Vakaviti-operated WhatsApp number (not directly to the operator), and Vakaviti
passes the enquiry on to the relevant operator. See `CENTRAL-ENQUIRY-OPERATING-MODEL.md` for the
operational detail behind this — this is a deliberate design, not a bug, but it is a genuine
limitation travellers should understand: Vakaviti is a relay in this flow, not the operator itself,
and cannot guarantee response time on the operator's behalf.
