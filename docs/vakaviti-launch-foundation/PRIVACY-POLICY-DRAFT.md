## 2. What Vakaviti is

Vakaviti is a directory of Fiji tourism operators (tours, activities, ground transport, and related
services) that helps travellers find and contact real operators. Vakaviti does not currently process
payments or confirm bookings — any booking made is between the traveller and the operator directly.

## 3. Information we collect

**From travellers:**
- If you submit a business claim or Founding Partner interest form: your name, email, and phone
  number, and (for claims) which business you're claiming.
- If you tap "Ask Vakaviti on WhatsApp" on a listing: we record which listing and operator the
  enquiry relates to, and the time. The WhatsApp message itself is composed on your own device and
  sent through WhatsApp — Vakaviti's servers do not see or store the message content.
- Standard web request data (e.g. IP address, browser type) may be logged by our hosting
  infrastructure (Cloudflare) as part of normal operation and security monitoring.
- Anonymous, non-identifying usage events for the deals section (which category was viewed, which
  deal was opened) — never tied to a name, email, or phone number.

**From operators/providers:**
- Business name, description, location, website, and contact details you or Vakaviti's review
  process record as part of listing your business.
- Evidence used to verify your business (e.g. the public source page checked, and when).

## 4. How we use this information

- To connect a traveller's enquiry with the right local operator.
- To follow up on a business claim or Founding Partner application.
- To maintain and improve the accuracy of operator/product listings (evidence-based verification).
- To operate, secure, and improve the Service generally.

We do not sell your information.
