Airport transfers with child seats: the short answer

You can request a baby or child seat for FJ$8 when you book a private transfer from Nadi Airport. Add each child’s age in special requests and our team confirms seat suitability and availability. The fare covers the whole vehicle (sedan up to 3 passengers and 3 bags, minivan 4–7 passengers and 7 bags, minibus 8–12 passengers and 14 bags), and your total, including extras, is shown at the Review step before you send your request. Your driver waits in the international arrivals hall with a sign showing your name.

Baby and child seats

  • Price. Child/baby seat: FJ$8, an optional add-on in the booking form, added once to your booking total (also on a return trip).
  • How to ask. Tick the child/baby seat extra and put each child’s age in special requests. Our team confirms seat suitability and availability with you.
  • Seat types. We do not list seat types on this page. If you need more than one seat, or a particular type, say so in special requests or ask our team on WhatsApp. The booking form has a single seat option, so our team confirms the applicable charge for any additional seat.

Supermarket stop, arrival and return journeys

  • Supermarket stop. A supermarket stop is a complimentary add-on in the booking form. For an ATM, bottle shop or pharmacy stop, say so in special requests and our team confirms.
  • Arrival. Your driver waits in the international arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. If you cannot find your driver, contact our team on WhatsApp.
  • Return journey. Choose a return trip and add your return date and time. Published return fares are lower than two one-way fares. Mention your child seat needs for the journey back in special requests too.
  • Late arrivals. Pickups late at night or very early in the morning can carry a surcharge; our team confirms the applicable charge with you.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a baby or child seat on a Nadi Airport transfer?

Yes, on request. A child/baby seat is an optional add-on in the booking form at FJ$8, added once to your booking total (also on a return trip). Add each child’s age in special requests and our team confirms seat suitability and availability.

How much is a child seat on a Nadi Airport transfer?

The booking form lists a child/baby seat at FJ$8, added once to the booking total, including on a return trip. The Review step shows your total, including the seat, before you send your request.

Which child seat types do you have?

We do not list seat types on this page. Tell us each child’s age in special requests and our team confirms which seat is suitable and available before your arrival. If you need more than one seat, or a particular type, say so in special requests or ask our team on WhatsApp. The booking form has a single seat option, so our team confirms the applicable charge for any additional seat.

Which vehicle do I need for a family with luggage?

The booking form is set up for: sedan 1–3 passengers and up to 3 bags; minivan 4–7 passengers and up to 7 bags; minibus 8–12 passengers and up to 14 bags. Fares are per vehicle, not per person. Tell us in special requests how many children and child seats you are bringing, and mention any pushchair or other bulky gear, so our team can confirm the vehicle suits.

Is the Nadi Airport transfer price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. The fare covers the whole vehicle within its passenger and bag limits. Your final total, including any extras, is shown at the Review step before you send your request.

Can we stop at a supermarket on the way from Nadi Airport?

Yes. A supermarket stop is a complimentary add-on in the booking form. For an ATM, bottle shop or pharmacy stop, say so in special requests and our team confirms.

Can I book a return transfer with child seats?

Yes. Choose a return trip in the booking form and add your return date and time. Return fares are lower than two one-way fares. Mention your child seat needs in special requests so our team has them for the journey back too.

Where will my driver meet us at Nadi Airport?

Your driver waits in the international arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. If you cannot find your driver, contact our team on WhatsApp.

Do I pay now to request a family transfer?

No payment is requested when you send your request. A real member of our Fiji-based team confirms the arrangements with you.

Are there extra charges for night arrivals or other extras?

Optional extras in the booking form are a child/baby seat (FJ$8, added once to the booking) and a surfboard or oversized item (FJ$24); a supermarket stop is a complimentary add-on. Pickups late at night or very early in the morning can carry a surcharge; our team confirms the applicable charge with you. A 10% discount is applied automatically to eligible standard transfer bookings when the transfer-only total is over FJ$50. Your total is shown at the Review step before you send your request; our team confirms the final charge, including any night surcharge.
