Frequently asked

How much does a Nadi Airport to Pearl South Pacific Resort transfer cost?

Current sedan fare is FJ$199 one-way (FJ$370 return), whole-vehicle — not per person. Minivan (FJ$269 one-way / FJ$500 return) and minibus (FJ$299 one-way / FJ$555 return) options are available for larger groups.

Approximately 145km, around 2 hours 14 minutes by road via Queens Road — one of the longer main-island transfers, reaching Pacific Harbour before the road continues on to Suva.

No. You reserve your transfer with no payment required at the time of booking. Payment is arranged directly with your driver.
