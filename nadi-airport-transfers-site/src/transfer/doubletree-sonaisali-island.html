Nadi Airport to DoubleTree Sonaisali Island — the short answer

A private sedan transfer from Nadi International Airport to the Sonaisali jetty is currently FJ$69 one-way, whole vehicle, not per person. The drive is approximately 18km, around 28 minutes via Queens Road and the Sonaisali turnoff. Your driver monitors your flight and adjusts pickup timing automatically if you're delayed. No payment is required to reserve — a real member of the local team confirms your pickup directly.
Road transfer vs. the island boat shuttle: DoubleTree Sonaisali is a small offshore island resort. We take you by private vehicle to the Sonaisali jetty on the mainland. From there, the resort runs its own short shuttle boat across to the island — that final leg is arranged directly by DoubleTree Sonaisali, not by us, so please confirm current boat shuttle timing with the resort when you book your stay.
