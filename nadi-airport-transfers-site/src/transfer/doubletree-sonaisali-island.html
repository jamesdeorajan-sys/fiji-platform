Nadi Airport to DoubleTree Sonaisali Island — the short answer

A private sedan transfer from Nadi International Airport to the Sonaisali jetty has a published fare of FJ$69 one-way, whole vehicle (sedan: 1–3 passengers), not per person. Your final total is shown at the booking Review step before you confirm. Our fare covers the road transfer only: it ends at the jetty on the mainland. The boat crossing to DoubleTree Sonaisali Island is arranged with the resort. No payment is taken when you request a transfer; a real member of our Fiji-based team confirms your pickup directly.
Road transfer vs. the island boat: DoubleTree Sonaisali is an island resort. We take you by private vehicle to the Sonaisali jetty on the mainland. The boat across to the island is arranged and run by the resort, not by us, and is not part of our fare — please confirm boat arrangements and timing with the resort when you book your stay.

Frequently asked

How much does a Nadi Airport to DoubleTree Sonaisali transfer cost?

The published sedan fare is FJ$69 one-way (whole-vehicle, not per person) for the road transfer to the Sonaisali jetty. Minivan (FJ$89) and minibus (FJ$119) options are available for larger groups. The resort’s boat is not included.

How long does the road transfer to the Sonaisali jetty take?

Journey time depends on traffic, road conditions and your exact pickup and drop-off point. We do not publish a fixed journey time for this route, so allow extra time around your flights.

Does the transfer include the boat to the island?

No. Our private vehicle takes you from Nadi International Airport to the Sonaisali jetty on the mainland. The boat across to the island is arranged and run by DoubleTree Sonaisali, not by us, and is not part of our fare. Please confirm boat arrangements and timing with the resort when you book your stay.

Is my final total the same as the fare shown on this page?

Not always. The fares on this page are published fares. Your final total is shown at the Review step of the booking form before you confirm: it uses the current live fare for your route, so it can be higher or lower than the published fare. A 10% discount is applied automatically when the transfer-only total (fare plus any extras, such as a child seat) is over FJ$50. Child seats are FJ$8 on request.

Which vehicle do I need for my group and luggage?

The booking form is set up for: sedan 1–3 passengers and up to 3 bags; minivan 4–7 passengers and up to 7 bags; minibus 8–12 passengers and up to 14 bags. Fares are per vehicle, not per person.

Can I add a child seat to my transfer?

A child-seat option is listed at FJ$8 in the booking form. Include your child’s age in special requests and ask our team to confirm seat suitability and availability.

Can I book a return transfer from the Sonaisali jetty to Nadi Airport?

Yes. Choose a return trip when you book and add your return date and time; the return total is shown at the Review step before you confirm. Our road transfer starts and ends at the Sonaisali jetty, so plan your boat crossing with the resort.

Where do I meet my driver at Nadi Airport?

Follow the meeting instructions in your transfer confirmation. If you cannot find your driver, contact our team on WhatsApp.

Do I pay now to book?

No. You can request your transfer with no payment at the time of booking. Our Fiji team confirms availability and payment options with you.

Who confirms my booking?

A real member of our Fiji-based team confirms your pickup directly — not an automated system.
