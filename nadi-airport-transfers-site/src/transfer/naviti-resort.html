Nadi Airport to The Naviti Resort — the short answer

A private sedan transfer from Nadi International Airport to The Naviti Resort is currently FJ$149 one-way (FJ$280 return), whole vehicle, not per person. The Naviti is approximately 100km, around 1 hour 38 minutes from the airport via Queens Road south. Your driver monitors your flight and adjusts pickup timing automatically if you're delayed. Frequently asked

How much does a Nadi Airport to The Naviti Resort transfer cost?

Current sedan fare is FJ$149 one-way (FJ$280 return), whole-vehicle — not per person. Minivan (FJ$189 one-way / FJ$350 return) and minibus (FJ$239 one-way / FJ$445 return) options are available for larger groups.

Is The Naviti the same as The Warwick?

No — they're two separate, adjacent resorts on the same stretch of Coral Coast beach, slightly further east than the Warwick. The Naviti is the family-focused sister property, with a kids' club and family pool complex. As near neighbours they currently publish the same fare tier and drive time, but each is booked to its own destination and its own driver drop-off.

How far is The Naviti from Nadi Airport?

Approximately 100km, around 1 hour 38 minutes by road via Queens Road south.

Do I pay now to book?

No. You reserve your transfer with no payment required at the time of booking. Payment is arranged directly with your driver.
