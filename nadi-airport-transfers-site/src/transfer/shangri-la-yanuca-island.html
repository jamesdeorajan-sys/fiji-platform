  • Whole vehicle, not per person. The booking form is set up for: sedan 1–3 passengers and up to 3 bags; minivan 4–7 passengers and up to 7 bags; minibus 8–12 passengers and up to 14 bags.

Frequently asked

How much does a Nadi Airport to Shangri-La Yanuca transfer cost?

The published sedan fare is FJ$129 one-way (FJ$240 return), whole-vehicle — not per person. Minivan (FJ$159 one-way / FJ$295 return) and minibus (FJ$199 one-way / FJ$370 return) options are available for larger groups.

How far is Shangri-La Yanuca from Nadi Airport?

The resort’s own directions (shangri-la.com) describe it as approximately 50 minutes by road from Nadi International Airport. Actual journey time depends on traffic and your pickup point, and we do not guarantee a journey time.

Is my final total the same as the fare shown on this page?

Not always. The fares on this page are published fares. Your final total is shown at the Review step of the booking form before you confirm: it uses the current live fare for your route, so it can be higher or lower than the published fare. A 10% discount is applied automatically when the transfer-only total (fare plus any extras, such as a child seat) is over FJ$50. Child seats are FJ$8 on request.

Which vehicle do I need for my group and luggage?

The booking form is set up for: sedan 1–3 passengers and up to 3 bags; minivan 4–7 passengers and up to 7 bags; minibus 8–12 passengers and up to 14 bags. Fares are per vehicle, not per person.

Can I add a child seat to my transfer?

A child-seat option is listed at FJ$8 in the booking form. Include your child’s age in special requests and ask our team to confirm seat suitability and availability.

Can I book a return transfer from Shangri-La Yanuca to Nadi Airport?

Yes. Choose a return trip when you book and add your return date and time. Return fares are listed on this page and are lower than two one-way fares.

Where do I meet my driver at Nadi Airport?

Follow the meeting instructions in your transfer confirmation. If you cannot find your driver, contact our team on WhatsApp.

Do I pay now to book?

No. You can request your transfer with no payment at the time of booking. Our Fiji team confirms availability and payment options with you.

Who confirms my booking?

A real member of our Fiji-based team confirms your pickup directly — not an automated system.
