Nadi Airport car rental questions

Who supplies my rental car?

Best Adventure Fiji supplies the rental vehicle and confirms availability, price, rental terms, payment and vehicle assistance. Nadi Airport Transfers (NAT) receives your request and coordinates your journey. NAT does not operate the rental business.

How much is car hire at Nadi Airport?

The price depends on your dates, vehicle, collection and return arrangements and the partner's terms. Ask for a written total in FJD that includes compulsory charges. No daily rate or vehicle availability is promised on this page.

Can I collect at Nadi Airport or request hotel delivery?

Tell us where you would like to collect and return the car. Best Adventure Fiji confirms whether those locations are available, any delivery charge and the exact meeting instructions. Do not assume an airport counter or terminal pickup is included.

Does sending a request confirm my rental?

No. A request starts a conversation and is saved with a reference number. Your rental is confirmed only after you accept the full offer and Best Adventure Fiji confirms the vehicle and arrangements. No payment is taken on this form; payment and any deposit are confirmed by the partner.

What should my rental quote include?

Ask for the rental total in FJD, compulsory charges, any security deposit, insurance cover and excess, fuel and mileage rules, permitted roads, cancellation terms, and collection and return instructions. Confirm the vehicle's passenger and luggage capacity for your group.

Can I request a family car or child seat?

Yes. Tell us how many people and bags you have and any child-seat needs, including the child's age. The partner confirms capacity, seat suitability, availability and any charges before you agree.

What if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number if you know it and tell the NAT team about changes. The rental partner confirms revised collection arrangements and any after-hours or waiting charges. Free waiting and automatic flight monitoring are not promised for rentals.

Should I book an airport transfer or a rental car?

Choose a private transfer if you want a driver for a specific journey. Request a rental if you want to drive yourself during your stay. You can also ask about an arrival transfer followed by a rental later; each service is quoted and confirmed separately.

How does WhatsApp support work?

If you agree on the form, a NAT team member creates a WhatsApp group with you and a Best Adventure Fiji rental agent. Group members can see your WhatsApp number. The partner handles the rental; NAT keeps the journey coordinated. Support hours and emergency assistance are confirmed with your offer.

What personal details do you ask for?

Your name, WhatsApp number, trip plans, driver age band and the country that issued your driving licence, plus an optional email and flight number. We do not ask for licence photos, passport details or card numbers on this form.

Tell us your plans. No payment now. The partner confirms the vehicle, price and terms.
