Vakaviti AI — Built in Fiji, Built for the Pacific

Vakaviti AI is the technology layer behind a growing network of honest, commission-free tourism sites across Fiji and the wider South Pacific. Every property in the network runs on the same principle: verified prices, real local operators, no pay-to-rank games.

Vakaviti means "the Fiji way." It started with one promise — that AI-powered travel guidance should work for the traveler and the local operator, not extract value from both to feed a platform. That promise now extends region-wide.

What Vakaviti AI Does

  • Lagi, the AI concierge — plans real trips, answers real questions, in seconds, for free

  • One technology, many islands — the same verification and AI infrastructure now powers every site in the network, not just Fiji
