# VAKAVITI CEO WAR ROOM — 30-DAY REVENUE & AI VISIBILITY RECOVERY

Status: EXECUTION ORDER DRAFT — Day 1–3 reconciliation authorized; production implementation remains stage-gated.
Date: 2026-08-15
North Star: **Profitable confirmed bookings per week**

## Objective
Convert the existing Fiji estate into one measurable commercial system without destroying proven assets or starting another unnecessary platform rebuild.

## Days 1–3 — Reconcile Before Changing
READ-ONLY for production systems. GREEN documentation/control-plane changes only.

Deliverables:
1. Master Asset Ledger: domain, host, repository/branch, production URL, database, payment path, GBP/GSC/analytics state, transactional status, dependencies, owner, risk and proposed tier.
2. Production Register: prove what code/version is actually live for FTT, NAT, Fiji Dash, ComeToFiji, Vakaviti/Lagi and priority specialist properties.
3. Commercial Truth conflict register: prices, add-ons, policies, currencies, ownership/operator, reviews/ratings, licence/insurance claims, booking semantics.
4. Revenue baseline: current booking sources, booking states, gross revenue, refunds/discounts and available cost/contribution evidence.
5. Funnel map: visitor → quote → booking request → accepted → paid/pay-later → confirmed → fulfilled → completed.
6. Google/AI visibility baseline for Tier-A candidates.
7. Risk register and rollback map for every RED system.

Exit gate: no P0 implementation until evidence is sufficient to identify the production owner and rollback for the affected system.

## Days 4–7 — Instrument and Correct Truth
Priority:
- implement minimum viable revenue-event taxonomy and persistent IDs
- connect landing/source/referrer/campaign to quote and booking where safely possible
- establish canonical commercial-truth schema and conflict workflow
- correct verified high-risk contradictions on Tier-A surfaces using reversible changes
- establish protected release/rollback discipline for critical branches/assets

Do not redesign sites broadly.

## Days 8–14 — Recover Conversion
Use measured funnel evidence to rank and fix actual leakage:
- booking-state integrity
- durable booking capture before handoff to WhatsApp/payment where applicable
- checkout/request clarity
- mobile conversion blockers
- price/policy clarity
- response/confirmation gaps
- return-transfer offer

Every change must have a before/after metric.

## Days 15–21 — Increase Traveller Value
Only after basic measurement is working:
- transfer → relevant tour cross-sell
- tour → relevant transfer cross-sell
- return-transfer capture
- contextual recommendations by destination/entity
- completed-trip → verified-review workflow design/initial implementation

Traveller suitability and trust outrank margin in recommendations.

## Days 22–30 — Amplify Proven Winners
- strengthen high-intent airport → resort route pages
- optimise highest-value FTT products with answer-first commercial structure
- wire ComeToFiji and specialist domains to canonical entities/products
- improve brand/entity relationships and structured data based on verified truth
- concentrate SEO/AI expansion on pages/products/routes with demonstrated commercial opportunity
- produce next 30/60/90-day plan from measured performance

## Non-Negotiables
- no invented commercial facts
- no duplicate independent fare authority
- no production RED change without explicit stage gate, tests, backup and rollback
- no counting booking requests as confirmed bookings
- no wholesale Fiji Dash merge
- no FTT migration for stack uniformity
- no broad South Pacific expansion during recovery
- no major new platform rebuild
- no vanity/cosmetic backlog outranking unresolved revenue/trust P0s

## Day-30 Success Definition
By Day 30 the CEO must be able to answer with evidence:
1. How many profitable confirmed bookings did we receive this week?
2. Which channel/domain/page/product/route produced them?
3. What gross revenue and contribution did they generate?
4. Where are high-intent travellers dropping out?
5. Which five interventions have the highest expected next-week contribution impact?
6. Which commercial facts are conflicting or stale?
7. Which assets deserve more investment and which should be held?

If those questions cannot be answered, the War Room is not complete regardless of how many features were shipped.
