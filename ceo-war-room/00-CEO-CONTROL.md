## Core Doctrine
**Facts have one authority. Experiences may have many perspectives.**

Deterministic systems own commercial facts such as prices, booking status and availability. AI handles language, reasoning, synthesis and recommendation over verified sources.

## Four CEO Ledgers
1. Asset Ledger — what exists, where, ownership, platform, production status, dependencies, risk and commercial tier.
2. Truth Ledger — canonical commercial/factual values, evidence, source, freshness, conflicts and consumers.
3. Decision Ledger — CEO decisions, evidence, status, rationale and supersession history.
4. Performance Ledger — traffic, source, quote, booking, revenue, contribution and loss signals.

## Evidence States
Every important assertion must be labelled as one of:
- FACT — supported by evidence
- CEO DECISION — strategic direction frozen until superseded
- HYPOTHESIS — requires measurement or validation
- OPEN QUESTION — unresolved and must not be represented as fact

## Decision Lifecycle
PROPOSED → APPROVED → IMPLEMENTING → VERIFIED → SUPERSEDED

Evidence may overturn a CEO decision. When that occurs, preserve the history and record the reason.

## Change Risk Classes
GREEN — reversible/non-production: documentation, specifications, tests, dashboards on copied/read-only data.

AMBER — production but reversible: metadata, structured data, verified copy corrections, attribution events, controlled links. Requires evidence and rollback.

RED — transaction-critical: fares, checkout/payment, booking state machine, dispatch, wallets, production database migrations. Requires explicit stage gate, tests, backup and rollback.
