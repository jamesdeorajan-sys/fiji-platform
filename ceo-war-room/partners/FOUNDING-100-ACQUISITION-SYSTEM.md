## Human gates — never automate away
Human approval is required before:
- claiming an operator is Vakaviti Verified
- publishing legal/licence/insurance claims
- agreeing commission or payout terms
- publishing an operator's non-public information
- activating payments/payouts
- publishing prices not confirmed by authoritative source/operator
- suspension/termination decisions
- high-risk activity acceptance where safety/licensing evidence is material

## AI agent roles
### 1. Scout Agent
Search approved public sources and internal portfolio references for Fiji operators/products. Produce candidates only; never auto-verify.

### 2. Entity Resolver
Deduplicate names, domains, Facebook/Instagram pages, phone numbers and operator/product aliases into canonical candidates.

### 3. Enrichment Agent
Extract public product/category/location/contact/booking facts with source provenance and timestamp. Mark uncertain fields explicitly.

### 4. Commercial Scoring Agent
Rank candidates using demand relevance, Western-corridor fit, transport attach opportunity, product quality signals, digital weakness/opportunity, likely booking readiness and verification burden.

### 5. Outreach Agent
Draft personalised WhatsApp/email/social outreach using only verified/public candidate facts. No autonomous sending until outreach policy is approved.

### 6. Onboarding Copilot
Turn operator replies, URLs and supplied documents into a proposed operator profile and product catalogue. Operator/human confirms factual/commercial fields.

### 7. Verification Copilot
Organise evidence, detect expiry/missing documents and prepare verification checklist. It recommends; authorised human/rules decide status.

### 8. Product Digitiser
Create structured product drafts: title, description, location, duration, inclusions, exclusions, suitability, capacity, pickup, price, cancellation and FAQs. Commercial fields require authoritative confirmation.

### 9. Distribution QA Agent
Check inventory completeness, canonical IDs, price semantics, landing pages, images/rights metadata and future Google Things to do readiness.

### 10. Partner Success Agent
After activation, monitor response/acceptance/fulfilment/review/payout signals and surface partners requiring human attention.

## Partner scoring
Use a transparent 100-point score; never let a black-box AI score be the sole reason to exclude a legitimate provider.

- transport attach potential: 15
- bookability/readiness: 15
- public trust/evidence quality: 10
- differentiated Fiji experience: 10
- digital distribution gap Vakaviti can solve: 10

## Sources to seed candidate discovery
Use multiple sources and preserve provenance. Examples include:
- Tourism Fiji experience listings and regional guides
- operator official websites
- Google Business/public web discovery
- Facebook/Instagram public business pages
- existing FTT/NAT/ComeToFiji/Vakaviti content and partner relationships
- tourism association/industry sources where available
- hotel/concierge recommendations
- traveller-demand/search data once reconciled

Do not scrape behind logins, evade platform restrictions, or treat directory inclusion as legal/business verification.

## Automation principle
AI may reduce the labour of creating a partner record from hours to minutes, but the system must never convert speed into fake trust. Every extracted fact carries source + timestamp + verification state.
