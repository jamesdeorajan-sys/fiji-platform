# AI-FIRST PARTNER COLLECTION PIPELINE

Status: PHASE-1 IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT
Date: 2026-08-17
Production effect: NONE

## Goal
Reduce operator pre-onboarding labour by 80–90% while preserving human control over trust, legal/commercial claims, pricing approval and activation.

## Pipeline
DISCOVER -> RESOLVE -> ENRICH -> PROVENANCE -> SCORE -> REVIEW -> OUTREACH -> CONFIRM -> VERIFY -> STRUCTURE -> DISTRIBUTE -> BOOK -> FULFIL -> PAY -> REVIEW

## Agent functions
1. Scout: find candidate Fiji operators/products from approved public sources and existing owned inventory.
2. Entity Resolver: deduplicate names/domains/social profiles and propose canonical operator/product matches.
3. Enrichment: extract candidate locations, products, durations, inclusions, policies, booking paths and contact methods.
4. Provenance: attach source, collection timestamp, confidence and exact claim context to every candidate fact.
5. Commercial Scorer: rank candidates by demand, inventory uniqueness, booking readiness, digital weakness/opportunity, transport attach potential, trust evidence and expected revenue leverage.
6. Outreach Composer: create personalised partner invitation based only on evidenced public facts.
7. Onboarding Copilot: pre-fill operator/product forms and ask only unresolved questions.
8. Verification Copilot: assemble evidence packet and expiry/recheck requirements; human decides verification.
9. Distribution QA: validate canonical facts and landing/booking paths before external syndication.
10. Partner Success: surface first-booking blockers, stale availability, cancellations and growth opportunities.

## Human-only gates
- Vakaviti Verified approval
- licensing/insurance acceptance
- bank/payout activation
- commission/commercial agreement
- canonical price approval where operator confirmation is required
- suspension for trust/safety reasons
- production activation of feeds or transaction-critical integrations

## Candidate scoring (100 points)
- demonstrated traveller demand: 20
- product uniqueness/quality: 15
- Western Fiji corridor relevance: 10
- transport attach potential: 15
- booking readiness: 10
- credible public evidence: 10
- digital distribution gap Vakaviti can solve: 10
- margin/contribution potential: 5
- responsiveness/contactability: 5

Do not use star ratings alone as a quality proxy.

## Initial sources
Priority source classes:
- existing owned Book Fiji Tours catalogue
- operator official websites
- Tourism Fiji public experience listings
- Google-visible official/operator pages
- public Facebook/Instagram pages as candidate evidence only
- existing Fiji Tour Transfers/provider relationships
- traveller questions and existing FAQ corpus

Third-party marketplace data may be used for discovery/comparison but must retain provenance and must not be relabelled as Vakaviti-verified data.

- fishing

## Founding cohort funnel
100 qualified candidates -> top 25 human-reviewed -> 10–20 recruited -> target 10 receiving completed bookings.

## Labour target
For a candidate with usable public information, machine processing should produce a structured evidence-backed onboarding packet in minutes, not require manual re-keying. Human effort should concentrate on ambiguous facts, trust verification, commercial agreement and relationship building.

## Success metrics
- minutes human labour per qualified candidate
- candidates structured/day
- % automatically deduplicated correctly
- outreach response rate
- operator confirmation rate
- time from discovery to distribution-ready
- active bookable operators
- operators with >=1 completed booking/month
- partner GMV
- contribution
- transport attach rate
- fulfilment/cancellation rate
- verified-review rate

## Safety/trust rule
AI can collect and recommend. It cannot turn an unsupported public claim into company truth.
