# Vakaviti.ai — BulaBooking brand architecture

## Strategic decision
Vakaviti.ai is the AI technology and innovation brand powering BulaBooking. Public BulaBooking experiences should identify the relationship as 'Powered by Vakaviti AI' where accurate and approved. BulaBooking is the traveller/operator-facing marketplace and promotional discovery brand.

## Recommended public language (subject to ownership verification)
'BulaBooking — Powered by Vakaviti AI.'
'Built for Fiji. Powered by Vakaviti AI. Connecting Fiji to the world.'
'A Fiji-focused, AI-powered booking and discovery platform.'
'Start Your Fiji Journey. Book Now with Confidence.' Booking requests remain pending until accepted.

## Legal/competitive claims gate
Do not publish 'Fiji's first', 'only Fiji booking platform', '#1 Fiji booking platform', 'Fiji-owned and operated', or '100% Fiji owned' as verified facts without legal entity evidence, operational verification, and dated independent competitive substantiation. Use aspirations internally, not unsupported public superlatives.

## Product separation
- BulaBooking: operator listings, tour/activity booking requests, authentic reviews, accommodation promotional referrals.
- Vakaviti.ai: AI-assisted operator content, discovery tooling, support and analytics, under clear human oversight.
- Accommodation partners: guest reserves and pays on partner's official website; BulaBooking tracks outbound referrals, not assumed completed bookings.
- NAT/FijiDash: separately disclosed affiliate for standalone transfers, no hidden preference or transfer inventory merging.
- Bula.app: optional future integration only with consent, data boundaries and approved commercial model.

## SEO and AI entity consistency
Publish accurate Organization metadata for both entities only after confirming official names, legal ownership, domain and public contact details. Cross-link official sites with clear 'powered by' relationship, maintain consistent descriptions, factual author/about pages, privacy and terms. Do not create fictitious awards, rankings, reviews or machine-generated endorsements.

## Launch gates
Verify ownership and permissions for Vakaviti.ai trademark/brand use, public corporate description and logo. Approve precise operator/guest-facing copy and ensure factual accuracy. No production deploy or DNS changes without CEO approval.
