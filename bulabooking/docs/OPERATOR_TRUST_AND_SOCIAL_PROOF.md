# Operator trust, verification and social proof

## Marketplace policy
BulaBooking may display qualifying operators as:
- Verified operator: platform identity and operating checks completed; scope and date of verification disclosed.
- Verification in progress: checks not completed; no verified badge.
- Not yet verified: public profile clearly discloses absence of verification.
- Suspended: profile and booking actions disabled under documented safety/compliance rules.

Unverified operators may have public discovery profiles and, only where eligibility/risk requirements permit, accept booking requests with prominent disclosure. Require minimum identity/contact, legal/safety compliance and operational suitability checks before allowing transactions. Do not imply unverified means unsafe or verified guarantees safety.

## Social proof
- Operator-supplied Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, TripAdvisor and website URLs are labelled external links.
- Links alone do not establish account ownership, customer ratings, follower counts or platform verification.
- Show only first-party verified completed-booking reviews and accurate counts from authoritative records. No fabricated review totals, star ratings, follower numbers or testimonials.
- External platform ratings require lawful access, source attribution, update timestamps and compliance with provider display policies; do not scrape or silently merge into BulaBooking stars.
- Link to public operator profiles with rel='noopener noreferrer nofollow ugc' and target='_blank' where appropriate; check links for malware/redirect risk before publication.
- Give operators control over which approved public links appear, while preventing impersonation.

## Public profile
Operator name, Fiji operating area, verification badge or neutral disclosure, services, FJD prices, actual verified-booking reviews, approved external social links, cancellation terms, and booking/support status.

## Anti-fraud and privacy
Staff-only verification evidence; never expose ID documents or guest contact details. Enforce account ownership checks, fraud flags, reporting/appeal process and audit logs. Profile edits that affect trust status require staff approval.

## Implementation gaps
This sprint adds domain helpers and tests only. It does not implement operator verification, account authentication, URL safety crawling, public profile pages, social APIs or live review aggregation. Those require staging integration and explicit release approval.
